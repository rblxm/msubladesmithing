Welcome In!!

We are a passionate group of students who are interested in art, craft, and science. Bladesmithing is a unique blend of these things that can really capture you. None of us expected to have a hobby like this, but we're sure glad we do!

How to Get Involved (Two Ways)

The first is casual. Come enjoy bladesmithing and exercise your creativity by working on your own projects. Pound, grind, heat, polish and sharpen metal to your heart's content! Busy one weekend? No worries, we'll catch you next time.

The second requires heavier commitment. Work with a small team to design and execute unique materials processes to create a blade for the TMS Bladesmithing competition. You really get to dive into the science and create something special.

No experience is required for either of these options and they can be mixed. If you are interested in hanging with us (yay!) please view our contact and meeting pages. See you soon :)
