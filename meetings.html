Meeting Information

Our regular meetings are held at two different locations depending on which step of the smithing process you are in. We have a workshop at an engineering club facility colloquially referred to as "Jolly". This is where you will forge and do most grinding. Thanks to our amazing advisor Dr. Johnson, we are grateful to have plenty access to the Materials Science teaching labs in the STEM building. It's in "2004" that you will heat treat, polish, and analyze your blade.

Club Dues

All members contribute $50 per academic year (covering Fall and Spring semesters). Club dues enable the activity of this awesome organization. They cover essential consumables like sanding belts, whetstones, grinding discs, propane, and steel. It also allows our club to stay healthy, by maintaining our equipment and purchasing cool new stuff for all members to use. Your support is greatly appreciated!
